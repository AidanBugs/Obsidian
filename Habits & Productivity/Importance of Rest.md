# Why is rest important?
In short, proper rests gives us the energy to tackle our tasks throughout the day as well as maintain good cognitive function and clarity.

# How to rest
First off, is doom scrolling rest? No. Playing games? No. Youtube? No. While these things may be fun or a way to pass the time they are not actually resting your brain. These sorts of things are a break from working but they are not actual rest.

In order to actually rest you need to relax your mind and body. 

- Go for a walk / run
- Meditate
- Power Nap
- Stretch
- Nonsleep Deep Rest (NSDR)

These are actual examples of resting. While doing these activities try to keep your mind clear and relax, free from distractions (phone) and it is also important to drink water too!
