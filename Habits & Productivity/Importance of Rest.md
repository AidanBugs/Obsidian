# Why is rest important?
In short, proper rests gives us the energy to tackle our tasks throughout the day as well as maintain good cognitive function and clarity. Rest is very important especially after extended periods of work such as long study sessions, exams, hackathons etc.

# How to rest
First off, is doom scrolling rest? No. Playing games? No. Youtube? No. While these things may be fun or a way to pass the time they are not actually resting your brain. These sorts of things are a break from working but they are not actual rest.

In order to actually rest you need to relax your mind and body. 

- Go for a walk / run
- Meditate
- Power Nap
- Stretch
- Nonsleep Deep Rest (NSDR)

These are actual examples of resting. While doing these activities try to keep your mind clear and relax, free from distractions (phone) and it is also important to drink water too! 

# Rest vs Breaks
Breaks are small rests away from smaller work sessions. Rest is closer to a full mental reset than a break which is a short term relaxation. For breaks, they are typically done in between working sessions. As such, it is important for breaks to not be more engaging than whatever you are working on, otherwise it will be difficult to start working again.
