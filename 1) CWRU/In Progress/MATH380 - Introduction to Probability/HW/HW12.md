# 9.7
$E(X_i)=1000,\sigma=\sqrt{Var(X_i)}=900$

$S=\sigma^2500_{i=1}X_i$

$E(S)=2500(1000),Var(S)=2500(900^2)$

$P(S>2500premium)=P(S-2500(1000)>2500(premium-1000))$

$P(S-2500(1000)>2500(premium-1000))\leq \frac{Var(X)}{(2500(premium-1000))^2}$

$=\frac{900^2}{2500(premium-1000)^2}$

We want this to be less than $0.001$

$0.001\geq \frac{900^2}{2500(premium-1000)^2}$

$\rightarrow(premium-1000)^2\geq324000$

$\rightarrow premium\geq\sqrt{324000}+1000$

For CLT:

$P(S\leq 2500premium)=P(\frac{S-2500(1000)}{50(900)}\leq \frac{2500(premium-1000)}{50(900)})$

$=\Phi(\frac{2500(premium-1000)}{50(900)})\geq 0.999$

$\rightarrow\frac{2500(premium-1000)}{50(900)}\geq 3.1$

$\rightarrow2500(premium-1000)\geq 3.1(50(900))$

$\rightarrow premium\geq 3.1(900)/50+1000$

$\rightarrow premium\geq 1055.8$

## a
$f_Y= \int_0^y \frac{x+y}{4} dx = \frac{y^2}8 + \frac{y^2}{4}=\frac{3y^2}8$ 

$f_{X|Y}(x|y)=\frac{f(x,y)}{f_Y(y)}=\frac23 \frac{x+y}{y^2}, 0< x< y < 2$

## b
$P(X<\frac12 | Y=1)= \int_0^\frac12 f_{X|Y}(x,1) dx =\frac23 \int_0^\frac12 x+1 dx$

$= \frac23 (\frac18 + \frac12)= \frac 5 {12}$

$P(X<\frac32 | Y=1)= \int_0^1 f_{X|Y}(x,1) dx =\frac23 \int_0^1 x+1 dx$

$= \frac23 (\frac12 + 1)= 1$

## c
$E(X^2)= \int x^2 f_X(x)dx$

> $f_X(x) = \int_x^2 \frac{x+y}4 dy$

> $f_X(x) = \frac12 + \frac 12 x-\frac38 x^2$

$E(X^2) = \int_0^2 \frac12x^2 + \frac12 x^3 \frac38 x^4 dx$

$E(X^2) = \frac{14}{15}$

On the other side

$E(X^2|Y=y) = \frac23 \int_0^y x^2 \frac{x+y}{y^2}dx= \frac7{18}y^2$

$\int_0^2 \frac7{18}y^2 (\frac38 y^2)dy=\frac{7}{48} (\frac{2^5}{5})=\frac{14}{15}$

They are equivalent!

# 10.15
## a

let $C_A$ denote the number of claims made by policy A holders.

$P(C_A\geq 5) = 1-\sum_{i=0}^4 P(C_A=i)$

> Let $C$ denote the total number of claims

> $P(C_A=i)= \sum_{c=0}^\infty P(C_A=i|C=c)P(C=c)$

> $=\sum_{c=i}^\infty \binom ci (\frac14^i)(\frac34^{c-i}) e^{-12}\frac{12^c}{c!}$

> $=(\frac14^i)e^{-12}\sum_{c=i}^\infty \binom ci (\frac34^{c-i}) \frac{12^c}{c!}$

> $=\frac{1}{i!}(\frac14^i)e^{-12}\sum_{c=i}^\infty \frac{c!}{(c-i)!} (\frac34^{c-i}) \frac{12^c}{c!}$

> $=\frac{12^i}{i!}(\frac14^i)e^{-12}\sum_{c=i}^\infty \frac{1}{(c-i)!} (\frac34^{c-i}) 12^{c-i}$

> $=\frac{3^i}{i!}e^{-12}\sum_{c=i}^\infty \frac{9^{c-i}}{(c-i)!}$

> $=\frac{3^i}{i!}e^{-12}e^9$

> $=\frac{3^i}{i!}e^{-3}$, $C_A\sim Poisson(3)$

$P(C_A\geq 5) = 1-\sum_{i=0}^4 \frac{3^i}{i!}e^{-3}$

## b
Let $C_B$ denote the number of claims made by policy B holders

We appply similar work to find that $C_B\sim Poisson(9)$ (flip $c-i$ and $i$ in the binomial expression)

Thus $P(C_B\geq 5) = 1-\sum_{i=0}^4 \frac{9^i}{i!}e^{-9}$

## c
