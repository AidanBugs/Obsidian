The bigger issue I have with the paper is because they compare all the hidden layers of model A to model B and take the pairwise max of the agreement as the similarity between the two models. My issue with this is that then as model sizes increase we are more likely to have more similar models simply because larger models = more hidden layers which lead to more chances to have similar pairs. This is why I think it would be slightly better to use the embedding matrix but I also think that doing this would be slightly different for LLM's since the matrices are sparse but thats irrelevant to this HTEC class. The key point between the papers is that they claim that AI models are converging on how they encode / understand our reality. My project is fundamentally different because it analyzes how the output / actions of AI models are similar/different to eachother. Since I am looking at benchmark performance, my project is analyzing AI output and how it performs on tasks rather than the other papers which focus on how AI understand words / things.
