# OSINT Digital Footprint Dashboard
OSINT short for open source intelligence is essentially a collection of "open" data sources that anyone can access. The idea is that anyone is able to find these informations online for free. The dashboard would be interesting to me as itt would allow me (and its users) to better understand how much of their data is public access, and really show firsthand the impact technology and our data has in our lives.

Again the disciplines being data science + sociology and interested in this topic because OSINT is a very cool double edged sword in the sense that on the one hand its truly fascinating how much data is out there but also incredibly scary. 

The deliverable would be this tool that would display how scary the interrconnected world truly is which we often don't notice through all the interconnectedness and QOL that comes with the digital age.
