Why can there be no theoretical phronesis

Phronesis is only involved in the action itself and not the end like techne. 

Additionally phronesis cannot be separated from the actor and is unique to each individual so cannot be used to create a universal theory. 

If you wanted to have a techne for a phronesis there must be a solid theory, but since there is no solid theory and is not universal theory it cannot be a techne.
