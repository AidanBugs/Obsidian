> Lent's research was titled "Predictors of two-months culture conversion among adult index TB cases in Kampala, Uganda". He spent the first bit of the presentation discussing what is TB and determining the severity of TB. They analyzed how Bandim TB score and Chest X-ray Features predict the two-months culture conversion. Lent looked at a variety of characteristics and used a t-test to determine if the characterstics had different means for if culture converged vs did not culture converged. Lent used LASSO regression to create optimal models for the Chest X-RAY and Bandim TB score. However, the models were not very good as the AUC was roughly 0.55 for both models meaning the models were slightly more accurate than a coin flip but not very. The models also had poor AIC and BIC indicating a very poor fit. Lent found that the most dominant predictor was the BaselineSmearGrade while disease extent was the second highest dominant predictor despite not being statistically significant. Using the models, they concluded that the Bandim TB score and the Chest X-ray are weak predictors of TB. 

> One of the things covered was the sufficient cause and necessary causes in diseases. Lent said that exposure to M.tuberculosis is a necessary cause but is not sufficient cause for development for TB. Lent also used a variety of statistics we learned such as Odd's Ratios, Risks Ratios, Risks etc which he used two sample t-tests to determine if the difference was significant.
