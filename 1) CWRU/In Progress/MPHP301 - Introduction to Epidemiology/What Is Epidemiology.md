# What is Epidemiology?
Epidemiology is concerned with the distribution and determinants of health events. Epidemiologic studies applied to control health problems in populations

# Uses of Epidemiology
- Historical
- Community Health
- Health Services
- Risk Assessment
- Disease Causality

# What is an Epidemic
The occurence of a region of cases of an illness / specific health-related behavior / other health related events clearly in excess of normal expectancy.

This means that normal expectancy can vary by region thus even if a region has a higher number of cases than other regions it doesn't necessarily indicate an epidemic.

# What is an Endemic
The habitual presence of a disease within a given geographic area or the usual occurence of a given disease within such an area. This is what determines the normal expectancy of a disease.

# What is a Pandemic
Pandemic is an epidemic that occurs on a global scale

# What is a Syndemic
Thie is the collision of two differennt epidemics. In other words, two epidemics that are happening simultaneously and may potentially be linked but not have to.

# Key Characteristics of Epidemiology
## Distribution
The occurence of diseases and other health events variies in populations with some groups affected more frequently than others. 

## Determinants
A determinant is a collective or individual risk factor that is causally related to a health condition outcome or other defined characteristics.

Poverty or less access to healthcase can be a determinant to poorer health outcome

## Exposures
Related to determinants, exposures pertain to contact to a disease causing factor or the amount of the factor that impinges upon a group or individuals.

## Outcomes
Result or possible results that stem from exposure to a causal factor

## Quantification
Statistics or counting of the health outcome.


# John Snow
John Snow mapped Cholera cases to a sewer map and it showed that people closer to a specific water pump had high rates of having Cholera so he developed the idea Cholera spreads through the water.

# Koch's Postulates
Koch proved that micro organisms can transfer diseases

1. The organism must be observed in every case of the disease
2. It must be isolated and grown in pure culture
3. The pure culture must when inoculated into a susceptible animal reproduce the disease
4. The organism must be observed and recovered from the expiremental animall.

# Framingham Study
Investigating aspects of health such as risk factors for coronary heart disease by looking at the data from a small city

# Epidemiologic Transition
Describes a shift in the patterns of morbidity and mortality from infectious diseases to chronic and degenerative diseases. 

Linked to a demographic transition

# Demographic Transition
High birth and death rate to low birth and death rates

# Operations Research
Defined as a typed of study of the placement of health services in a community and the optimum utilization of services. Epidemiology helps provide quantitative information regarding availability and cost of healthcare services
