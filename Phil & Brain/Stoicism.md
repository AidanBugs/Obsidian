# What is Stoicism?
Stoicism is a philisophical framework of ethics which laid out how to live a virtuous life. In general, it can be seen as accepting what you cannot change and change what you have control of. Stoicism focuses on the development of self control and fortitude as a means of overcoming destructive emotions in an attempt to use inner calm and clear judgement to reach the ultimate goal of freedom from suffering.
