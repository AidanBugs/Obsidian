# Imposter Syndrom
## What is Imposter Syndrome
In short, imposter syndrome is a feeling that you are underqualified for something that you are doing. This is a common feeling among programmers where they feel like they don't actually know what they are doing a lot of the time. In general it is about appearing smarter or better than how one actually feels. However, since the way people view "smarts" or competence can greatly very, there are different ways that imposter syndrom can manifest in people:

- **Superhero**
    - The superhero type comes from viewing competence as being able to handle a variety of roles. For example juggling work, house chores, passion project, learning skills and overall trying to excel in all of these areas simultaneously. People who fall victim to this kind of imposter syndrome might feel shame if they start to fall behind in any of the areas they try to juggle.
- **Natural Genius**
    - The natural genius type falls under how quickly things are done / learned with the least amount of effort possible. In general, it is the idea of having natural talent at things without needing to put the effort into it. For people who suffer from this, the factt that effort is needed to learn is a sign of failure / shame regardless of the actual yield of their efforts.
- **Perfectionist**
    - This type stems from how a task is done and executed, making artwork perfect for example. For this type, the slightest flaw is shame. This could be getting a 99/100 instead of the perfect 100.
- **Soloist**
    - Simply put if the task isn't done alone then it isn't worth much. This type tends to put an excessive emphasis on the idea that they can do everything alone as opposed to being able to rely on others.
- **Expert**
    - Similar to perfectionist, however the expert focuses on knowing how to do everything. Not knowing the smallest details is a sign of shame.
